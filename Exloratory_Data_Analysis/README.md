# Exploratory Data Analytics (EDA)

This section focuses on understanding the raw structure, scope, and shape of the data 
before diving into deeper analysis. EDA answers: *what data do I have, and what does 
it look like?*

## Contents

### 1. Database Exploration
Understand the overall database: tables, schemas, relationships, and how data 
connects across the system.

### 2. Dimension Exploration
Explore categorical/descriptive fields (e.g., customer, product, region) to 
understand the unique values, hierarchies, and groupings available for analysis.

### 3. Data Exploration
General inspection of the dataset — row counts, data types, null values, 
duplicates, and overall data quality checks.

### 4. Measure Exploration
Explore numeric/quantitative fields (e.g., sales, revenue, quantity) — their 
ranges, distributions, and basic statistics (min, max, avg, sum).

### 5. Magnitude
Compare the size of a measure across different categories 
(e.g., total sales by country, total quantity by product).

### 6. Ranking (Top N - Bottom N)
Identify top and bottom performers within a category 
(e.g., top 5 products by revenue, bottom 5 customers by orders).

## Goal
By the end of this section, you should have a clear picture of the dataset's 
structure and be ready to move into deeper, trend-based analysis.
