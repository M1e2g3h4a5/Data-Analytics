# Advanced Analytics

This section builds on the exploratory findings to uncover trends, patterns, 
and deeper business insights over time and across segments.

## Contents

### 1. Change Over Time
Analyze how a measure changes across time periods (daily, monthly, yearly) 
to identify trends, seasonality, and growth patterns.

### 2. Cumulative Analysis
Track running totals or moving averages over time to understand 
accumulated performance (e.g., running total sales, cumulative growth).

### 3. Performance Analysis
Compare current performance against a target, benchmark, or previous 
period (e.g., year-over-year growth, actual vs. average).

### 4. Part-to-Whole
Analyze how individual categories contribute to the overall total 
(e.g., % of total sales by region or product category).

### 5. Data Segmentation
Group data into meaningful segments or buckets based on specific criteria 
(e.g., customer segments by spending, products by cost range).

### 6. Reporting
Consolidate insights from all previous analyses into structured reports 
or dashboards for stakeholders and decision-making.

## Goal
By the end of this section, you should be able to explain not just *what* 
the data shows, but *why* it's changing and *what actions* it suggests.
